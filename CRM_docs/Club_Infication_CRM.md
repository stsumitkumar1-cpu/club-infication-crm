Club Infication CRM 
CRM • Functional Requirements & Role Access 
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 
1. Purpose 
Club Infication needs a simple CRM to manage customer records, membership plans, payments, usage, 
refunds, sales, and executive incentives in one place. 
The system will replace the existing Excel-based process and make it easier to manage and track all customer 
information. 
Customers will not have login access to the CRM. Customer information will be entered and managed by Club 
Infication's Executives and Managers. 
2. User Roles 
The CRM will have three user roles: 
Super Admin 
The Super Admin will have access to the complete system. 
Key features: 
• View and manage all customer records 
• Manage Managers and Executives 
• View all membership plans 
• View payments, pending amounts and refunds 
• View customer usage 
• View sales and performance 
• Manage executive incentive rules/calculations 
• Search and filter all customer records 
• View overall reports and business information 
Manager 
A Manager will manage the Executives assigned to them. 
Key features: 
• View their assigned Executives 
• View customer records entered by their Executives 
• View customer membership and payment details 
• View pending amounts and refunds 
• View customer days/nights usage 
• View team sales and performance 
• View executive incentive information 
• Search customers within their team 
A Manager will not have access to customers or Executives belonging to another Manager. 
 
 
Executive 
Executives will be responsible for entering and managing customer data. 
Key features: 
• Add new customer records 
• Enter purchased membership/plan details 
• Enter payment information 
• View their own customer records 
• View customer membership details 
• View paid and pending amounts 
• Record customer usage 
• View days and nights remaining 
• View their sales and incentive information 
An Executive will only have access to the customers/data assigned to them. 
3. Customer Data Management 
Customer information will be stored as data within the CRM. Customers will not have their own CRM login. 
Customer records can include: 
• Customer name 
• Phone number 
• Email 
• Membership ID 
• Purchased plan 
• Plan amount 
• Amount paid 
• Pending amount 
• Membership validity 
• Days and nights included 
• Days and nights used 
• Days and nights remaining 
• Assigned Executive 
• Assigned Manager 
• Refund information 
4. Existing Excel Data Import 
Club Infication currently has customer information stored in old Excel/XLS files. 
This existing data will be imported into the CRM. 
The import will include available information such as: 
• Customer details 
• Membership ID 
• Previous/purchased plans 
• Current/active plans 
 
 
• Plan amount 
• Payment information 
• Membership validity 
• Used days/nights 
• Remaining days/nights 
• Assigned Manager/Executive 
The system should maintain both previous plan history and the customer's current plan, where applicable. 
This will allow the team to continue working with existing customers without manually entering all old records 
again. 
5. Membership & Plan Management 
The CRM will store the membership plan purchased by each customer. 
For example: 
Plan Amount: ₹90,000 
Benefit: 3 Nights / 4 Days 
Validity: 5 Years 
Different plans can have different prices, validity periods, days, and nights. 
The customer's current active plan should be clearly visible. 
6. Payment & Pending Amount 
For each customer, the CRM will show: 
• Total plan amount 
• Amount paid 
• Pending amount 
• Payment history 
• Refund amount 
This will allow the team to quickly understand the customer's financial status. 
7. Customer Usage 
The CRM will track how much of the membership a customer has used. 
It will show: 
• Total days 
• Days used 
• Days remaining 
• Total nights 
• Nights used 
• Nights remaining 
 
 
8. Refund Management 
Refunds will be recorded against the relevant customer and membership. 
The system will maintain: 
• Refund amount 
• Refund date 
• Reason 
• Related customer/membership 
• Refund history 
9. Customer Search & Filters 
Authorized users will be able to find customer records using: 
• Membership ID 
• Customer name 
• Phone number 
• Email 
• Executive 
• Manager 
• Plan 
• Membership status 
10. Executive Incentives 
The CRM will calculate Executive incentives based on the sales amount achieved. 
Club Infication will provide the final incentive calculation rules and slabs. 
The system will show: 
• Executive sales 
• Target/achievement 
• Eligible sales 
• Incentive earned 
• Incentive paid/pending 
Refunds or cancellations can be considered in the incentive calculation based on the final business rules. 
11. Email Notification 
When an Executive adds a new customer, the system can automatically send an email to the customer's 
registered email address containing the relevant membership information. 
12. Dashboards 
Super Admin Dashboard 
Overall business information including: 
• Total customers 
 
 
• New customers 
• Total sales 
• Pending payments 
• Refunds 
• Active memberships 
• Customer usage 
• Executive performance 
• Incentives 
Manager Dashboard 
Information related to: 
• Their Executives 
• Their customers 
• Team sales 
• Pending payments 
• Customer usage 
• Incentives 
Executive Dashboard 
Information related to: 
• Their customers 
• Sales 
• Payments 
• Pending amounts 
• Customer usage 
• Incentives

Super Admin

Manager 
↓
